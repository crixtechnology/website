# Training + Internship: text for each program length (copy into Admin)

Each of the 30 programs below has its own text. Open **Admin → Training + Internship**, search the card (e.g. "MERN 1 month"), and paste each block into the field with the same name. Leave every other field (price, stipend, duration, eligibility…) as it is.

**Admin can edit only 4 of these fields:** Title, Short description, Skills, Responsibilities. The rest — card points, What you'll learn, Who it's for, Before you start, and FAQs — have no field in Admin; they are listed under "Not in Admin" for reference, and only reach the site through the startup step in PR #147.

> ⚠️ If you change a program's **Short description** in Admin before PR #147 is merged, that step skips the program, so its card points, What you'll learn, Who it's for, Before you start and FAQs stay old/empty.
